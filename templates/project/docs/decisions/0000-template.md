# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decided by: name

## Context
What forced a decision? Constraints, risks, and what you knew at the time.

## Options considered
1. Option A: pros, cons.
2. Option B: pros, cons.

## Decision
What was chosen, in one or two sentences.

## Why
The reasoning someone will need in a year, including what would make you revisit it.

## Consequences
What gets easier, what gets harder, what must now be true.

## Revisit when
An event or date.
